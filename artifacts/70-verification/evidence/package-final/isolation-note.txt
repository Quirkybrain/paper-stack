== Evidence isolation note (wp-50) ==
First invocation of packaging/verify-deb.sh and the gtk-launch check was passed a
*relative* evidence directory; XDG_DATA_HOME/XDG_CACHE_HOME must be absolute, so the
application resolved its data/cache to the user profile
(~/.local/share/com.quirkybrain.paperstack, pre-existing since 2026-10-07) and a
session tab from the smoke run was restored in the gtk-launch screenshot.
Both checks were re-run with absolute paths; final evidence in the parent directory
was produced by the isolated re-run. First-invocation screenshots/logs are retained
byte-for-byte under superseded/first-invocation/ for audit. The smoke conclusions
(About/license/notices/clean close) are independent of session state and were
re-confirmed by the re-run. User-profile app data was not modified by choice of
cleanup (pre-existing, test-app state; left untouched beyond the run's own writes).
